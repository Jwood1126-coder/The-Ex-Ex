## Part 0 — The conception: THE PIN AND THE PEN (the Signal)

One breath: **a boy who cannot tell his mother he is alive spends fifty years building
himself a voice, and sends the message when everyone it was for is dead — to us.**

The book is about **reaching people, and the lag between sending and arriving.** In 1838
this is not a metaphor; it is the period's physics: knowledge travels at the speed of a
ship, news arrives six to twelve months old or never, and the squadron is a probe beyond
real-time contact with Earth. Every major character is a signal and its degradation:

- **Charlie** is mute at planetary scale — illiterate, flogged over letters [whose
  letters ⚑ TYBTM verify in flight], his mother a quarter-mile from his screaming and
  unreachable by any means he owns. The pin at the skylight is **the one message that
  would have arrived instantly** — on the weather roll, to the chart-maker at his table
  — and he refuses to send it. The memoir is the slow version, fifty years in the
  building, dispatched when Wilkes (d. 1877) and the mother are past receiving:
  addressed, therefore, "To the Crew of the Ship Universal."
- **Reynolds** is the licensed channel gone underground: the secret journal kept against
  a jamming order, every chapter implicitly a letter to Lydia, the instrument curdling
  from love-letter to dossier as the voyage corrupts the correspondent.
- **Wilkes** wants to own the channel — journals banned, courts self-convened, the
  Narrative his monument — and starves on a dead channel himself: the one uncorrupted
  transmission he ever received was Bowditch's blessing on his charts, and the only
  reply the universe ever posts him is the Upshur sentence. Both rule-11 readings are
  transmission readings: the tyrant jamming his crew's voices *is* the age's most devoted
  signal-sender, whose charts still save lives in 1943. Never netted.
- **Vendovi** is the Signal's conscience and its limit: seized as a message — a hostage
  posted to "American justice" — carried two years as evidence, and never once allowed
  to transmit. No one of standing ever takes his testimony down. His untaken statement
  is the loudest silence in the book: the sounding never taken, in a man.
- **The nation** is the dead receiver: the country at the pier in 1842 is not the
  country that posted the squadron in 1838. The charts only truly arrive in November
  1943, at Tarawa, as gift and casualty report in the same tide.

**The three ranges.** The one act — reaching another person — is staged at three
distances, and the book's texture alternates among them: *across a gunwale* (the
encounter chapters, run on the Mirror grammar: can we see anything but our own fear
wearing the other's face?); *across an ocean* (the mail, the journal, Lydia, the
mother); *across time* (the memoir, the charts, the reader). The Second Signing binds
them with a lived question: knowing how rarely the reaching succeeds and what the
failures cost, do you sign for another crossing?

**The reader splits (kept live to the last page, by structure):** the memoir is love
completing its arc / self-forgiveness dressed as confession / proof that transmission is
impossible and writing is what humans do instead of arriving / the only true message is
the one never sent. Openness governs the wanting and the writing only — **never the
killing**: Malolo is adjudicated once, by the narrator-umpire, in the book's only spoken
verdict.

**The epic register** is unchanged from v0.1 (the Odyssean shape, the catalog energy,
nostos denied, the invocation displaced to the last page) — now with its engine named:
this epic's sea is *distance itself*, and every mailbag, chart, and journal page is an
oar against it.

## Part 1 — The frame (SETTLED by the theme: F2, the two documents)

The Signal is F2 with a heart. Charlie's chapters ride the 1890 memoir; Reynolds'
chapters ride the dated secret journal; the press enters Act VI as the third, public
document. The pin pays three times (prologue stops before the choice; the Atlantic
chapter completes the night; the desk supplies the words). New under v0.2: the frame's
present tense is **the physical act of composition** — the self-taught hand, the
cramp, the crossed-out addressees — per film law #6 (the talky climax needs a body).

declared here and in the author's note.

## Part 2 — POV plan (movement-scale, unchanged; theme duties added)

As v0.1, with each camera now carrying a Signal duty: Reynolds' sections are *written
objects* (entries with dates, jeopardy of discovery, the addressee drifting from Lydia
to the dossier); Charlie's sections are *spoken-into-being* (the old man's voice
reconstructing the boy's senses, wonder-asides anchored 1890); the press chapters are
*broadcast* (no addressee at all — what the world hears instead of the truth). Wilkes
never POV; Vendovi observed constant, never interior, his thread built strictly from
witnessed action [T1 under witness-class mechanics].

   transmitter power (charts, surveys, the Depot) because no one will simply answer him.
2. Charlie's Boston; the mother [name ⚑ in flight]; poverty as the recruiting officer.

### PART VI — THE RECKONING (1841–42) — the trial as the war of documents
29. The long way home; the pennant hauled down unwatched [E]. *Nostos denied begins.*
30. Homecoming to the dead receiver: the *Vincennes*' lonely June 10; the fleet home
    piecemeal [E — Niles' June 11/25]. **Vendovi dies the day after landfall** [E];
    **the crates cross-cut** [T2 — his death against the collections entering the
    Patent Office; the portrait and, later, the skull: two records of one man, never
    filed for the reader]. Theme: arrival kills the message; the museum receives what
    the nation ordered.
31. The lectern: Wilkes at the National Institution, vindicating the discoveries,
    Poinsett present [E — Niles' June 25 ⚑ date]. Theme: transmission raised to full
    power before the court can answer.
32. **The trial** — the documents converge: reciprocal prosecutions; Wilkes requested
    the court [E ⚑ roster]; journals as exhibits [⚑ GAP 4]; Paulding's private orders
    published mid-trial [E]; **the four marines suing as citizens** [E — Niles' Sept 17
    ⚑ outcome]; the verdict in secret session, in transit [E]. Theme: every channel the
    commander closed reopens in a courtroom — the paper trail turning on its keeper.
33. **The sentence, verbatim** [E — Upshur, Sept 22 1842; one OCR clause ⚑]: "a command
    far above the just claims of your rank… the rights of others of her citizens… the
    mildest form." Theme: the only answer the universe ever posts him — the blessing's
    exact inverse, delivered through the rank wound. *The shadow's atonement: a shrug.*
34. Codas, ledger open: the Smithsonian seed (Smithson's words); Reynolds' afterlife —
    the counter-record's keeper rises into the institution [⚑ career verification in
    flight]; Whippy's consulship ⚑; **Tarawa, once, at full length** [E — act_5:92;
    mechanism ⚑]: the charts arriving a century late, as rescue and casualty report in
    the same tide. The signal outlives every sender.

### CODA — 1890. The desk. The second signing.
The composition staged as action [T1 — GAP 2 floor in flight]: the self-taught hand;
the pin chapter reached; **the blank page** [T1] — he tries to write Vendovi's face,
memory returns only Agate's portrait, and he writes the blank and says that it is one;
the confession's words chosen while the reader — the night's only other witness —
watches the gap between what they saw and what he sets down ("the devil took possession
of me" [V — TYBTM] against the dedication); addressees considered and crossed out;
then, in full: **"To the Crew of the Ship Universal."** The second signing. The book in
the reader's hands is the signal arriving; what they have received — a love letter, an
alibi, or beautiful noise — is left to them. *Return with the elixir; the invocation
arrives last.*
