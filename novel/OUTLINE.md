## Part 0 — The conception: THE PIN AND THE PEN

One sentence: **an illiterate boy carries a belaying pin to his sleeping commander's
skylight and puts it back; fifty years later, in letters he taught himself, he writes the
book you are holding — and the voyage between those two nights is the story of every man
aboard choosing what to do with the wound the Navy handed him.**

The epic Jake asked for is real and load-bearing, but it is an *American* epic, which
means the ledger rides along: the boon at the bottom of the world is a continent, and the
price is Malolo, and the book never nets one against the other. So the hero's journey here
is deliberately **inverted at its two hinges**:

1. **The threshold trial is a refusal.** In the classic shape the hero's first victory is
   over a monster. Charlie's first victory is over *himself* — the pin night, eleven days
   out. The monster he declines to become is the book's antagonist-shape: the wound passed
   downward (Braid 2). Everything Wilkes does for four years is the road Charlie didn't
   take, walked to its end by the man who flogged him.
2. **The elixir is testimony, not treasure.** The voyage's treasure (charts, specimens, a
   continent) comes home to a court-martial and a country that shrugs. What the hero
   actually brings back — the thing the tribe lacked — is the *counter-record*: the pen
   learned late, the memoir dedicated "To the Crew of the Ship Universal" [E — TYBTM front
   matter]. The pin becomes the pen. That is the monomyth's return-with-the-elixir, played
   honest.

Three journeys braid through the one voyage:

| traveler | journey | shape |
|---|---|---|
| **Charlie Erskine** (the body) | the hero's journey proper, both hinges inverted | wound → refusal → trials → dead world → witnessing the wound at full scale → return → the pen |
| **Charles Wilkes** (the weather) | the shadow epic — the same monomyth run by a man who seizes the boon and refuses every gift of the return | promised world withheld → self-crowned → apotheosis at the ice → the wound passed down at Malolo and Honolulu → nostos denied → the reprimand as his "atonement with the father" |
| **William Reynolds** (the pen aboard) | the apostasy arc — believer → heretic → witness | worship → the five minutes → the secret journal as counter-scripture → the trial where paper testifies |

Wilkes is **never POV** (three-windows rule). His journey is legible only through what the
other two see, what he names on the map, and what he writes in fear — which is exactly
rule 11 enforced by structure: read one way he is the epic's hero; read the other, its
warning. Both readings must survive every scene.

**The epic register** comes from five sources, not from inflation: (a) the Odyssean shape
— a fleet, a decade, a homecoming; (b) the catalog energy of musters, stores, and
instruments (the *equipment-and-the-age* harvest is the armory scene's fuel); (c) the
scale honestly earned — 87,000 miles, 280 islands, a continent [VERIFY exact figures
against Wilkes' summary before use]; (d) **nostos denied** — the epic where the kingdom
does not want them back, the feast replaced by a court; (e) the invocation displaced to
the end: the muse arrives on the last page, when the illiterate man writes.

## Part 1 — The frame (PROVISIONAL: F2, "the two documents")

Adopted from 05 Part III ★ / BS-2, pending Jake's ruling (Part VI decision 1) and the
Erskine-coverage answer (the architecture-grade unknown, dossier 11).

- **Charlie's chapters ride the 1890 memoir** — retrospective, an old man in Boston
  writing toward the one page he has never said aloud. The wonder-aside gets its
  in-fiction anchor: old Erskine answering across time.
- **Reynolds' chapters ride the secret journal** — dated, close-present, written under the
  Sept 13 order that forbids it [E — act_2 ch. 8]; the standing tension of discovery costs
  nothing in invention because the prohibition is documented.
- **The two documents converge at the trial**, where the voyage's paper — required
  journals, demanded correspondence, the written opinions collected in the ice — comes
  back as evidence [VERIFY mechanics — GAP 4], and the press becomes a third, public
  document (the `press-harvest` layer: the Upshur sentence verbatim, the leaked Paulding
  instructions, the marines' suits).
- **The pin pays three times** (05 ★): the 1890 prologue shows the old man and the pin and
  *stops before the choice*; the chronological scene completes the night in Part II; the
  1890 close supplies the confession. One object, three payments.

Fallback if F2 falls: F1 (frame returns at act joints) preserves ~80% of this outline
unchanged; only the narration's fabric changes, not the beat map.

## Part 4 — The liberties register (what invention is licensed here)

Every creative liberty this outline takes, named, with its gate class:

1. **The two-documents frame** [I — licensed]: the *conceit* is invention; every fact
   inside it (the Sept 13 order, the memoir's existence, the late literacy, the
   dedication) is [E]. The invention is arrangement, not event.
2. **Malolo-as-news** [I — conditional]: gated on the witness-whereabouts check. If either
   witness was *at* Malolo, the design flips to direct witness and the news-beat dies.
3. **Charlie's February traverse camera** [I → E pending]: he was aboard [E]; the *scenes*
   await the Erskine read-through. If TYBTM starves it, the traverse runs on the
   generic-forecastle camera (02 step 1) — grounded in [W], no invented events.
4. **Connective tissue** [I — licensed under 02's three conditions]: watches, meals,
   weather, movement between attested beats. Never a new event, never a dated claim.
5. **Utiroa's flat voice** [craft, not fact]: a treatment choice (05 ★), reversible.
6. **The monomyth mapping itself** [architecture]: stations are scaffolding for the
   author's eye and never appear in the text — no character discovers a "threshold"; the
   reader must be able to read the whole book without noticing Campbell was ever here.
   (Same rule as T2 modern knowledge: shapes scenes, never named.)

**Not licensed, anywhere:** invented dialogue attributed to real persons beyond attested
reported speech; Wilkes interiority; Vendovi interiority; any event contradicting the
record; netting the ledgers; softening Malolo or inflating it.
