## Part I — Verified extracts

### 1. The Honolulu floggings have names and arithmetic — vol 4, Appendix, ~p. 544–545 ★★ E

Wilkes' own General Order, verbatim (U.S. Ship *Vincennes*, Harbour of Honolulu, October
28th, 1840):

> "The naval court-martial now in session, having sentenced Michael Ward, John Riley, and
> Peter Sweeny, and the sentences having been approved by me, except so far as they are
> mitigated in the two first cases, will be carried into effect in the following manner.
> Michael Ward will be punished with one dozen lashes on his bare back with the
> cat-o'-nine-tails, alongside each of the three vessels of the squadron now in port. John
> Riley will receive sixteen lashes alongside of each of the vessels… Peter Sweeny will be
> punished with eight lashes alongside of each of the vessels… The launch of the Vincennes
> will be rigged with a platform and gallows, and a guard of marines, with a boatswain and
> three mates, to guard the prisoners and inflict the punishment. The crews of the vessels
> will be dressed, and their rigging and sides manned, at 10 A.M. on Friday, at which time
> the punishment will be inflicted. — CHARLES WILKES, Commanding Exploring Expedition."

Same page: Acting-Master's Mate A. M. Cissery of the *Peacock* dismissed the service "for
violating the rules and regulations by giving the crew grog."

**What this settles and refines:**
- **Grounding debt #4 settled with names: Ward (12×3 = 36), Riley (16×3 = 48), Sweeny
  (8×3 = 24) — flogged through the fleet**, on a launch rigged with platform and gallows,
  alongside each ship in turn, all crews mustered to witness. The staging is richer and
  worse than the base's generic gratings — a *traveling* punishment.
- **Fact-refinement candidate for act_5 ch. 28 ⚑:** the base says "by the commander's sole
  order… absent a court-martial." Wilkes' own document shows a **Wilkes-convened
  court-martial** issuing these sentences, "approved by me." The 1842 charge turned on the
  *legality of that court and those sentences* (a squadron-commander authority Wilkes
  arguably didn't hold) — a subtler and more damning mechanism than a bare order: he built
  legal theater around the lash. Reconcile with the court-martial record and Erskine's
  eyewitness before rewording the base. [VERIFY against trial record]
- Note the date (Oct 28, 1840) sits at the re-enlistment crisis exactly as the base has it.

### 2. The capitulation crawl is Fijian law, in Wilkes' own ethnography — vol 3, ~p. 86  ·  E ★

> "If a town is compelled to entreat to be permitted to capitulate, for the purpose of
> saving the lives of its people, its chiefs and principal inhabitants are required to
> crawl towards their conquerors upon their hands and knees, suing for pardon and imploring
> mercy…" (running head: "CUSTOMS OF THE FEEJEE GROUP.")

This is the *general custom* section, written before Malolo occurs in the volume — it
sources the base's "made the submission their own law prescribed for the utterly conquered"
(act_5 ch. 27) precisely, and gives the novel the terrible extra clauses the base withheld
(the offered daughters; the sacrificial victims; "a whole population is sometimes butchered
in cold blood"). Ledger discipline applies: this is Wilkes explaining Fijian law — use with
the archive test in hand.

### 3. Piner's Bay is named for a signal quartermaster — vol 2, ~p. [head TBD]  ·  E/S ★

> "…too much wind at this time to tack; I therefore had recourse to luffing the vessel up
> in the wind, and wore her short round on her heel. At the same time we sounded, and found
> a hard bottom at the depth of no more than thirty fathoms. I made a rough sketch of this
> bay, which I have called Piner's Bay, **after the signal quarter-master of that name**…"

- **Cast find:** the bay at the heart of the terrified-prose night is named for an enlisted
  man — Piner, signal quartermaster, *Vincennes* — one more named sailor for the Class-2
  roster, and a map-name (window one) that honors *downward* for once. [VERIFY spelling
  against scan and muster.]
- **Seamanship gold (S):** "wore her short round on her heel"; hard bottom at thirty
  fathoms inside the bay — the sounding that proved land.
