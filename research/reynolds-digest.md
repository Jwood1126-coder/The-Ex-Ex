## Ch. 1 — To the South Atlantic

- **[15] The sailing entry** ★ V/E — full text confirmed, including the continuation the
  base trims: "…and they; oh! shield them from harm, give them health & peace, grant them
  every blessing, and in mercy to us, allow them all once more to see the wanderers from
  the firesides." The base (act_1 ch. 7) ends at "…better than all the things of Earth."
  The trimmed half is the prayer *for those left behind* — scene-bank seed for any
  F2-frame chapter.
- **[15] "Here we are, just one half of the Squadron"** E — early squadron separation
  (Jake's margin query "I thought only the relief went to rio" — resolve during full read).
- **[15] The Sept 13 journal order, verbatim** ★ E/V — including "to be used, if they
  possess any merit" (the Navy's own dismissiveness, in its own words) and Reynolds'
  declaration of the second journal. Matches base ch. 8; page now known.
- **[15] Porter's hanging, Sept 15** ★ E/S/V — "caught around the neck by one of the small
  ropes, and swung off! A fearful spectacle! Literally hung!… as his face blackened, our
  hearts were chilled with horror." Scene-bank primary seed.
- **[17] Madeira landfall Sept 16; Peacock anchors next morning** E — "strange that we
  should have crossed the Ocean without seeing each other & yet have been so near."
- **[17] Left Madeira Sept 25** E. **[17] Shoal-hunting from Oct 1** E/S — the Bonetta
  Shoal; the Captain's breakfast remark that shoal-hunting "gives an excitement to
  navigation" ★ V (a rare *pleasant* Wilkes-at-table seed); trumpet warnings aloft;
  flying fish "myriads of them, like flights of swallows"; Drayton drew, Pickering
  dissected.
- **[18] Bioluminescence, Oct 5–6, off St. Jago** ★ S/V — full passage incl. "the night
  being black as Erebus" and "Even the idlers were lured up from their haunts below," and
  the detail the base doesn't use: **"My middle watch that night"** — the glowing sea was
  Reynolds' middle watch, same witching hours as Charlie's pin. [NOVEL] symmetry, free.
- **[18] The Danish brig, 28 days from Rio** E — "right glad… to have intelligence of the
  American Exploring Expedition conveyed to that quarter of the world" — knowledge at the
  speed of a ship, in his own words (throughline seed).

## Ch. 6 — To Valparaíso
- **[82] Mistaken for the captain of an English merchantman** V — "Me! this child!! who am

## Ch. 7 — The Pacific Islands
- **[86] "We regretted exceedingly that we could not land, particularly because the other
  Islanders had been so hostile"** E — base ch. 18 uses this; page now known.
- **[86] "The worshipful Skipper of this craft… He has received a bitter dose, &"** ★ E/V —
  the "bitter dose" line is *Reynolds mocking Wilkes* (Wilkes received the dose in
  officer correspondence); the base (ch. 18) deploys "bitter doses" as the officers
  receiving Wilkes' reprimands — **inverted attribution; check the full passage and fix or
  re-attribute** → `fact:`/`voice:` candidate. ⚑
- **[87] "But enough of this! I had intended never to introduce such themes, among these
  pages. Once, I fondly…"** E/V — the self-censorship plant (base ch. 18 ✓); the cut-off
  "Once, I fondly" begs the full sentence — full-read target.
- **[88] The lagoon-island Sunday** ★ S/V — several hundred islanders, the trees "as a
  screen, a drop curtain," the lagoon "created for no impurer use than to yield up the
  Pearls…" (base ch. 18 ✓; page known).

## Ch. 9 — Tutuila, Upolu, and Australia
- **[107] Five days in the boats** ★ S — "even my bones were sore… To quail or shrink or
  tire would not do" (base ch. 18 ✓; page known).
- **[108] The dance in the hut** ★ S/V — full passage (base ch. 19 ✓).
- **[109] "…the valuable attendance of my black valet, David Smith"** ★ E — David Smith's

- **[109] Tuetila, verbatim** ★★ E/V — confirms the base's account *and* the exact
  wording: "Captain Wilkes refused to receive him, much to our surprise & mortification.
  Why, we could not tell! 'Twas not politic & was rude & insulting." Also: **Underwood
  carried the news** ("Mr. Underwood mentioned his arrival to the Captain") — the
  braid-junction wiring is primary-sourced. Soft spot settled pending Jake's page check.
- **[111] The suspension, verbatim** ★★ E/V — new details the base lacks: the order came

  leeward" trying to account for it; then a second order "to confine myself to my
  apartment"; **he polled Lieutenant Case and Mr. Knox on the forecastle — "Not five
  minutes," they said.** Names for the base's "two brother officers" → `fact:` candidate.
- **[112] The charge wording** E — "in an improper & disrespectful manner & set a bad
  example to the crew. I knew in my very heart that…" — cut-off; full-read target (the

- **[116] Sydney transportation-society passage** E/V — base ch. 20 ✓; page known.
- **[117] The fête row and "foul scamp," verbatim** ★★ E/V — **PROJECT_NOTES §3 soft spot
