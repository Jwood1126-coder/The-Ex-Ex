# THE EX. EX. — Base Narrative

A complete reference narrative of the United States Exploring Expedition (1838–1842), written in a single voice, to serve as the master source for the novel.

## What this is

This is not the novel. It is the **spine**: the whole story, in order, in one voice, with the best primary-source quotes embedded where they belong. When we write the novel, we write *from this* — no more juggling Philbrick, Erskine, Reynolds, Wilkes, and a folder of notes mid-sentence.

**Fact status:** dates, names, positions, ship data, casualty figures, and afterlives have been checked against the standard record — Stanton's *Great United States Exploring Expedition*, DANFS ship histories, the Smithsonian's Ex. Ex. digital collections, USNI *Proceedings* (the Tarawa chart claim), and the current state of the secondary literature. Where sources conflict (e.g., total deaths: 28 by the strict count, "close to forty" by looser ones; Malolo's dead: 74–104), the text states the range or the conservative figure. A handful of scene-level details rest on your own transcriptions from Erskine and Reynolds — confirm page numbers during your primary-source read-throughs, and anything that fails inspection dies by commit.

## Provenance key

- Acts I–II are **refined from your existing drafts** (2015–2020 Google Docs). Your scenes, your structure, your best lines kept — tightened, fact-corrected (e.g., the Rio commodore was **Nicolson**, not Downes), and completed where placeholders stood.
- Acts III–V are **new**, written in the same voice from the historical record (Erskine's *Twenty Years Before the Mast*, Reynolds' *Private Journal*, Wilkes' *Narrative*, with Stanton and Philbrick as navigation charts — consulted for facts, never for prose).
- Quotes attributed to Erskine, Reynolds, or Wilkes are from public-domain primary sources or your own transcriptions; confirm exact wording and page numbers during read-throughs.

## Workflow

1. Push this to the GitHub repo: `https://github.com/Jwood1126-coder/The-Ex-Ex`
2. As you read the trinity (Erskine → Reynolds journal → Reynolds letters), correct or enrich by commit — the commit history becomes the fact-check log.
3. When the base is stable, we branch: `novel/` directory, one file per chapter, dual-POV per the editorial brief. The base narrative stays frozen as the reference.

## The one rule

The base narrative is allowed to be told; the novel must be shown. Don't polish this document into the novel by accident — its job is to be *complete and correct*, not beautiful. (It's allowed to be beautiful anyway. It mostly is.)
